# The foundation: set it up now, not after the grant (decided by Piotr, 17.09.2026)

The application submitted today says the foundation is founded in September–October 2026 and entered
in KRS by the end of the year, with its first fiscal year closed on 31.12.2026. This file is what that
takes, why it is worth doing before any grant decision, and what it does not change about the ING prize.

## What registration unlocks, from the day of the KRS entry

- **Donations with a tax deduction for the donor.** A person deducts donations up to 6% of income
  (PIT, art. 26 ust. 1 pkt 9), a company up to 10% (CIT, art. 18 ust. 1 pkt 1), for donations to an
  NGO pursuing public-benefit purposes (art. 4 ustawy o działalności pożytku publicznego). **OPP status
  is not required for this.** Before the entry exists, nobody can give the project money except as a
  private gift to a person, which no company will do.
- **A legal entity for agreements**: a partnership with UW under art. 39 ustawy wdrożeniowej (UW
  publishes an open call for a partner, at least 21 days; the partner must exist at that moment),
  CSR agreements, school pilots, a public collection (zbiórka publiczna) or a Patronite/zrzutka page
  run by an organisation rather than a person.
- **Grant eligibility that needs no turnover history**: FERS 05.01 social-innovation incubators
  (microgrants 50–120 tys. zł, 100% funded), Fundacja PFR, Fundacja Orange, Fundacja Empiria i Wiedza
  (BGK), mPotęga (mBank), MEN open competitions (NGO status required), NOWEFIO (call every
  November–December for the following year), Erasmus+ KA210 (no seniority requirement).
- **Two clocks that start at the entry and cannot be accelerated later**: OPP status needs two years
  of uninterrupted activity (art. 22 ust. 1 UDPP) and is what enables the 1,5% PIT designation;
  Erasmus+ KA220 coordination needs two years of legal existence. An entry in November 2026 means
  both open in November 2028; an entry in April 2027 means May 2029.
- **A closed fiscal year.** With the first fiscal year ending 31.12.2026 and its financial statement
  approved by 30.06.2027, the foundation has a "closed year" for the 2027–2028 calls that ask for one.
  The turnover will be tiny, so the big FERS/NCBR calls still go through a UW-led consortium, but the
  formal box is ticked. (The accounting act allows merging a short first year with the next one; we
  deliberately do not use that option.)
- **CIT**: income spent on statutory purposes in science and education is exempt (art. 17 ust. 1
  pkt 4 CIT), so donations and grants are not taxed on the way in.

## What it does not change about the ING prize

- The rules define a participant as a **Start-up** (JDG, spółka cywilna, spółka handlowa, spółdzielnia)
  or a **Młody Naukowiec**. A foundation is neither, so it cannot be the applicant, and the application
  filed today as a young scientist's team stays exactly as filed.
- A team's prize is **split equally and paid to the members' personal bank accounts**; ING adds the 10%
  flat tax on top and pays it. Getting that money into the foundation is a donation by each member.
  The personal-tax side of that step (the prize itself is taxed by lump sum; a member's donation is
  deductible only against their other income) needs an accountant before the final on 25.11, and it is
  a fair question for ING's mentoring, since ING wrote the rules.

## Decisions to make before the notary

| decision | recommendation |
|---|---|
| founder(s) (fundator) | one natural person suffices; the founder may sit on the board. One founder keeps the notarial act simple; four founders make the founding an act of the whole team. Either works. |
| name | check KRS (ekrs.ms.gov.pl) and CEIDG for collisions and the domain; "Fundacja EdMat" |
| seat | Warsaw; a flat is fine with the owner's written consent |
| founding fund (fundusz założycielski) | no statutory minimum; ≥ 1 000 zł is customary. If business activity is ever registered, ≥ 1 000 zł must be earmarked for it (art. 5 ust. 5 ustawy o fundacjach). |
| governance | zarząd (1–3 people) plus a separate **rada fundacji** as the supervisory body: not required now, but required for OPP status later, and a statute change costs another KRS filing. Board and council members must not be related to each other for OPP. |
| purposes | written in the language of art. 4 UDPP: nauka, szkolnictwo wyższe, edukacja, oświata i wychowanie (pkt 14); działalność na rzecz osób w wieku emerytalnym (pkt 10); działalność wspomagająca rozwój techniki, wynalazczości i innowacyjności (pkt 12); działalność na rzecz dzieci i młodzieży (pkt 15a). This wording is what donors' deductions and grant calls check. |
| paid activity | list both **nieodpłatna** and **odpłatna działalność pożytku publicznego** in the statute. Paid public-benefit activity lets the foundation charge for the test generator or a workshop **at cost** without registering business activity. When the generator is meant to earn a surplus, register działalność gospodarcza then (a second KRS register, a statute change, full books). |
| fiscal year | calendar year; the statute or the accounting policy states that the first fiscal year ends 31.12.2026 |
| ministry | the statute names the supervising minister (for a science-and-education foundation: the minister for education, or for science and higher education); the annual activity report goes there |
