## 0. How much to trust it

The prompt asked for URLs, quoted passages and a verified / reported / unknown label per answer.
The report gives **no URLs, no quotes, and labels 28 of 29 answers "Verified"**. Statute numbers,
paragraph numbers and prices are the kind of detail a model produces fluently and gets subtly wrong.
So: every claim below is graded by what I can corroborate from my own knowledge, and everything
that would change money, law or a default in the product gets a "verify with" column. Nothing in
this report is evidence for a lawyer or a tender; it is a map of what to look up.

Grades: **A** consistent with what I know and with public primary sources; **B** plausible, one
detail I cannot confirm; **C** doubtful or contradicts what I know; **?** no way to check.

## 3. What must go to a lawyer before any school sees this

1. **§ 22 signature list.** Does the archive accept **podpis zaufany**? If yes, zero cost; if only qualified/osobisty, budget ~1 000 PLN/year per school for a seal or require the director to hold a qualified signature.
2. **Deprived vs limited parental authority** and the default scope for each; what document the school may demand.
3. **Adult pupils**: may the school keep informing parents until the pupil objects, or is consent required from day one?
4. **DPIA sign-off**: who signs for the pilot school (its IOD) and whether our pre-filled template is acceptable to them.
5. **Pilot legality**: confirm that a satellite-module pilot (cafeteria, library, meetings) needs no organ prowadzący consent, and what a full switch requires mid-year.
