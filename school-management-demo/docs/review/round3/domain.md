# Round 3 — domain, pedagogy and legal consistency of the R1–R7 documents

Lens: do the seven documents written after the Gemini triage (`docs/GUARDIANS.md`, `docs/RETENTION.md`,
`docs/ARCHIVE.md`, `docs/compliance/README.md` + the three generated documents, `docs/SIO.md`,
`docs/IMPORT.md`, `docs/PUSH.md`) describe the code that exists, and is what the code does defensible
under Polish school law and school practice?

Method: findings were checked against the code, and the load-bearing ones reproduced on a live seeded
server (`EDMAT_DATA=… node server/index.js`, demo day 23.10.2026) rather than inferred from prose. The
three compliance documents were fetched in full in both locales
(`GET /api/compliance/{dpia,accessibility,dpa}?locale={pl,en}`); the archive package was generated and
its XML parsed; the store was diffed against the retention table with a script; the guardian matrix
was walked with real logins (registrar, both Kowalczyk parents, the class teacher, the nurse). Where
a finding says „reproduced", the request and the response are in the discrepancy column.

Severity: **blocker** — the document asserts something untrue about a legal obligation, or the code
discloses data to someone the document says cannot see it; **major** — the school would have to work
around it, or would sign/publish a document containing a false statement; **minor** — inaccuracy,
inconsistency or a gap that has to be closed before a pilot but harms nobody today.

Reference: `docs/research/2026-09-23-gemini-triage.md` (claims and grades), closed findings in
`docs/review/pedagogy.md` and `docs/review/README.md`. Nothing here repeats a finding closed there.

### 1.1 Two questions the brief asked that produced no finding

**Is the accessibility statement's structure the statutory one?** Yes, with one gap. The eight
headings map onto the model from the ustawa o dostępności cyfrowej: status pod względem zgodności
(§ 2), data publikacji i ostatniej istotnej aktualizacji (§ 1, as fill-ins), metoda oceny — samoocena
(§ 3), treści niedostępne z uzasadnieniem (§ 4), ułatwienia i skróty (§ 5), informacje zwrotne i dane
kontaktowe (§ 6), postępowanie odwoławcze (§ 7), dostępność architektoniczna i aplikacja mobilna
(§ 8). The feedback and enforcement paragraphs are correct and complete: the right to request, the
alternative-form request, **7 dni** for the response, **2 miesiące** as the maximum extension, the
skarga to the podmiot and then the wniosek to the **RPO**. Missing: an explicit „Data sporządzenia
deklaracji" as its own filled field rather than a phrase inside the method sentence, the link to the
declaration's own page in BIP, and a link to the wniosek/skarga procedure. Two inaccuracies are
listed above as D3-39 (PFRON) and D3-40 (keyboard).

**Is a document-only pupil treated consistently across the register, the transcript and SIO?** The
printed artefacts are consistent; only the XML is not. The księga (`registry.js:324–335`) returns
`pesel: null` plus `identityDocument` plus `identityLabel` — no empty PESEL field, pupil present. The
odpis arkusza ocen prints `ID.identityLabel(s)` → „paszport FL123456 (UA)" (`registry.js:288–291`).
The certificate (`homeroom.js:497–518`), the honours certificate and the duplicate sheet
(`registry.js:771`) carry no identity number at all, so a document-only pupil is indistinguishable
from any other. No path refuses or omits the pupil. The one inconsistency is the SIO `<uczen>`
element — D3-46.

## 2. Decisions the team must take

Not bugs. Each is a choice the code currently makes silently, and each needs an owner and — for the
first four — the lawyer already named in `docs/research/2026-09-23-gemini-triage.md` § 3.

1. **What „zakończenie roku szkolnego" means for § 22** (D3-22, D3-18). End of teaching (last Friday
   after 20 June) or 31 August? The answer moves the archive deadline by two months and decides
   whether the August retake results are inside or outside the sealed package. It also decides the
   retention clock boundary. Until it is answered, every date the director is shown is a guess.
2. **Is a court-restricted parent still a recipient?** (D3-01, D3-05.) The doc says `none` means
   nothing. But a parent stripped of authority may still have to be told about a trip their child
   goes on, and the school may want a record that the *zawiadomienie o zagrożeniu* was sent to both
   parents. The team must decide per notification kind, not globally: the fix in D3-01 assumes „never
   notify" and that is a policy call, not a bug fix.
3. **Adult pupil: which mode ships?** `config.adultAccess` defaults to `until-objection`
   (`server/seed/00-base.js:29`). The prototype's default is the opposite of what UODO practice
   suggests. Deck question 7. D3-11 adds a wrinkle: whichever mode ships, a consent recorded under
   one mode must not be silently reused under the other.
4. **Is the archive package category A, or is it the container of B5/B50 content?** (D3-15.) The
   answer decides whether `archives` grows without bound in the store and whether the school's own
   JRWA sees the copy in the logbook as documentation at all.
5. **Coverage policy for the 46 uncovered collections** (D3-13, § 3). Three positions are defensible:
   assign every collection a class; declare the module collections „not documentation, operational
   only, Bc"; or declare that retention covers only the statutory documentation and say so in the
   doc. What is not defensible is the current state, where the omission is invisible.
6. **Does push ship at all in the pilot?** (D3-27, D3-33, D3-34.) With push off, the DPA's
   no-third-country sentence is true and the lock-screen findings are moot. With push on, the school
   acquires a US sub-processor and needs the transfer basis in the DPA. This is a product decision
   that changes a signed document, not a switch.
7. **Who may give the recording consent** (D3-03). The code currently asks „is this account in
   `parentIds`". The lawful test is „does this person hold parental authority". Once D3-03 is fixed
   the deprived parent cannot consent — but nor can he *refuse*, and the other parent's consent then
   suffices. That asymmetry should be deliberate.
8. **What the school does about the pickup list** (D3-12). Linking `careAuthorizedPickups` to guardian
   accounts turns a paper authorisation into a system object. That may be more than the school wants;
   the alternative is a printed warning on the care screen.
9. **Does a pupil's identity number change, or does the pupil?** (D3-45, D3-48.) Adding a PESEL,
   changing a surname and correcting a birth date are all „the same person, new data" — but `s.id`
   is a name slug and the login is derived from the name, so the system currently treats a name as
   immutable. Either the id and login stop deriving from the name, or every name change is a
   documented, audited, mirrored operation. Until one of the two is chosen, neither the mid-year
   PESEL nor the court-ordered surname change has a lawful path.
10. **Does a re-issued certificate overwrite or version?** (D3-48.) `POST /api/homeroom/report-cards`
    updates the `documents` row in place. A school must be able to say what it issued, to whom and
    when; a duplicate is a separate document with its own number. This is a records decision before
    it is a code change.
11. **Strict or lenient imports?** (D3-50, D3-51, D3-52.) CSV/JSON rejects the whole file on an
    unknown entity; aSc/Optivum silently drops the row. Both are defensible; having both, undocumented,
    in the same endpoint is not. Pick one model and make `docs/IMPORT.md` describe it.
