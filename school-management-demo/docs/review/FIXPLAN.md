## Product decisions taken for the agents

- **S-10 pupil-record read scope.** Grades, remarks, homework submissions and descriptive assessments are readable by: the pupil, their guardians with `accessScope: 'full'`, teachers who teach that pupil's class or group in that subject, the homeroom teacher (all subjects), the principal, and the support team (counselor, psychologist, specialEducator, speechTherapist, supportTeacher). Cafeteria, library, after-school care, nurse and DPO do **not** read grade records; they keep the pupil directory, attendance presence for the day, and their own module data. Guardians with `accessScope: 'info'` see attendance, timetable, messages and announcements but not grades; `'none'` sees nothing.
- **Time.** All school logic runs in `Europe/Warsaw`; `config.timezone` defaults to it; the container sets `TZ`. Client sends local times, server stores ISO with offset.
- **Storage.** Keep zero dependencies. Per-collection files with dirty tracking are the target; the single-file format stays readable (migration on first start). The documented threshold for moving to SQLite stays in `docs/STORAGE.md`.
