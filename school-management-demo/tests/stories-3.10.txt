3.10.1 As an Event Organiser, I want to draft an event brief with its objective, a measurable outcome and the non-negotiable constraints, before any room or rota is planned.
3.10.2 As an Event Organiser, I want every event room planned by layout, so seated capacity, the accessibility buffer and the venue's fire limit are checked against the expected attendance.
3.10.3 As an Event Organiser, I want a shift rota per station that shows which shifts are still short of helpers.
3.10.4 As a Student Volunteer, I want to claim a shift only once my guardian's consent and the safety briefing are on record, so nobody starts a shift unprepared.
3.10.5 As a Safeguarding Lead, I want shifts for volunteers under 16 refused when they exceed the daily hour cap, fall into curfew hours, leave no rest gap, or have no adult on the same shift.
3.10.6 As an Event Organiser, I want briefing documents published in tiers, so parents, helpers and organisers each see only what their role needs.
3.10.7 As a Gate Volunteer, I want to scan visitor passes while the hall has no network, and have the queued scans settle when it returns.
3.10.8 As a Gate Volunteer, I want a re-scanned pass to report "already inside" and the same pass seen at two gates to report a collision, instead of silently admitting twice.
3.10.9 As a Catering Lead, I want headcount and allergen totals as aggregates only, so no attendee-level dietary list leaves the system.
3.10.10 As an Event Organiser, I want approval refused while any risk still scores in the unacceptable band of the register.
3.10.11 As an Event Organiser, I want a printable run sheet carrying the schedule, room capacities, staffing and the residual risks.
3.10.12 As a Data Protection Officer, I want event passes, scans and dietary records purged 30 days after the event while the aggregate counts survive.
