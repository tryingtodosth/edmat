# EdMat — the 2026-09-09 update for webek4.fuw.edu.pl / edmat.net

**Scope: a routine, purely ADDITIVE update automated by `deploy/fuw/*.sh`.** Twenty-three
migrations across ten apps, every one of them adding a column, a table or a choice — nothing
dropped, nothing rewritten in place; one data step (`events.0007` seeds an `EventStaff` host row
for every existing event — idempotent, milliseconds). No new environment variables, no new Python
packages, no Apache/vhost change. The frontend gained one dependency (Tiptap, MIT), already inside
the built bundle.

**If the 2026-08-28 `-e` bundle (`main @ 5dfec99`) was the last applied, this contains everything
since: nine commits, `f79064d` … `6ca6ce2`.** It is the whole first pass of the lifelong-learning
programme (`AUDIENCE-BRIEF.md`; root `CLAUDE.md` §17AL–§17AT): the audience widens to toddlers
(via an adult), school pupils, seniors and career changers; the events area becomes a conference
tool; and a set of cross-cutting rules follows.

## What is in it

- **Audience bands** (§17AL) — one `audience` value on every content model (`early_years` /
  `primary` / `secondary` / `university` / `adult` / `senior` / `all`; every existing row is
  `university`), `?audience=` narrowing every browse list, a chip row on the homepage, a required
  band on every submit form, a badge on every card.
- **Events as a conference tool** (§17AM–§17AO) — organisers/reviewers/volunteers, a multi-day
  `runs_until`, tracks + sessions + speakers + links, bookmarks and `/events/agenda` with `.ics`
  export, per-session Q&A; registration modes (rsvp / approval / form with the organiser's own
  questions), **a waiting list instead of a refusal** (first asked, first offered, 24 h claim,
  lazy expiry), check-in, session seats, CSV export; a call for contributions with single-blind
  review and scheduling straight onto the programme. Report kinds `event` and `contribution`.
- **Guardian accounts** (§17AP) — birth year asked at registration (never stored), under-16 sent
  to a guardian, `Guardianship` + `POST /api/auth/children/`; a minor's account cannot message,
  be public, upload an avatar, geocode, list tutoring or host; **comments and posts by minors,
  and pictures posted into a minor band, are HELD** as report-queue rows a moderator restores.
  **The login form now takes an email OR a username.**
- **Sorting and the content-language rule** (§17AQ) — `?sort=` on exercise lists; lists narrowed
  to the reader's languages with the hidden count in `X-EdMat-Hidden-Languages` and a "Show them"
  notice. **Day-one consequence: an English-interface visitor sees 743 of 746 exercises hidden
  behind the notice** — the rule working, not an outage (§17AQ records the decision).
- **Pictures and small PDFs on comments** (§17AR) — `CommentAttachment`, ≤ 3 per comment,
  ≤ 5 MB, pictures re-encoded to WebP, PDFs scanned when ClamAV is reachable; stored under
  `media/comment-attachments/` (Django creates it on first upload — `www-data` must own
  `media/`, which the apply script already checks).
- **A rich-text editor** (§17AS) — Tiptap, lazily loaded, Editor/Source modes remembered on
  the profile; storage unchanged. Sanitizer widened to `figure`/`figcaption` and own-media `<img>`.
  **Comments are now sanitized on write** (they never were) and rendered through the maths
  pipeline.
- **Reading comfort** (§17AT) — a header "Aa" text-size button, high contrast in Settings, a
  44 px floor on controls, hero copy for every age.

## Steps

Exactly the table in `deploy/fuw/README.md`:

1. On this VM (already done for this bundle): `bash deploy/fuw/pack.sh` → `FUW/UPDATE-20260909.tar.gz`.
2. On the host: `bash deploy/fuw/transfer.sh FUW/UPDATE-20260909.tar.gz`.
3. On webek4 as `todoonet`:
   `tar xzf UPDATE-20260909.tar.gz && bash UPDATE-20260909/deploy/fuw/apply-on-server.sh`
   — backup, rsync, pip (no-op), migrate, permissions, Apache restart, smoke test. The smoke
   gained six checks for this release's seams (audience on exercise rows, the hidden-languages
   header, `sort=`, the agenda and children routes answering 401 rather than 404, `registration_mode`
   on events).
4. Authenticated smoke: `EDMAT_EMAIL=… EDMAT_PASSWORD=… bash UPDATE-20260909/deploy/fuw/smoke.sh`.

## After it is up — what to look at by hand

- The homepage: the audience chip row under the hero; the "N items in other languages are
  hidden — Show them" notice for an English-interface visitor; the "Aa" button in the header.
- Register a test account with a birth year under 16 — it must refuse and point to a guardian;
  then, signed in as an adult, Settings → "Children" → add one, and sign in as it by username.
- An event you host: the Programme section, the registration mode radios on its edit form, the
  "Call for contributions" panel.
- A comment with a picture attached; the Editor/Source toggle above the comment box.

## Known consequences worth restating

- The English-interface homepage opens on almost everything hidden (the corpus is Polish). The
  banner is the intended answer for now; machine-translation drafts are the real fix and remain
  a follow-up (§17AQ).
- `Exercise.audience` is `university` for every corpus row, so the `primary`/`senior` chips
  return nothing until somebody submits content for those bands. Also intended.
- Held minor content appears in the moderation queue as a report filed by its own author with a
  fixed reason — restore publishes it (§17AP).
